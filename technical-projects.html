Ecology Simulation

JavaScript, HTML Canvas, no dependencies

Run it live · Source (MIT) · runs in your browser, best on a desktop

  • I wanted to see whether a world I could imagine would do anything interesting if I built it. Small square organisms sense, eat, sleep, fight, flee and breed in a 2D world with a day and night cycle. Seeded, so any run can be replayed.
  • The design is mine: eleven inherited genes, senses that shrink at night, a behaviour priority stack, and homes that form at a well-fed sleep, drift toward where an organism eats, and get abandoned after attacks. There is no aggression gene; violence comes from hunger, relative power and opportunity.
  • Claude Code wrote most of the implementation to my direction. I designed it, tuned it and tested it until the ecology held together at any world size, about 900 organisms at 2 ms a tick.
  • Part art piece, part experiment in what AI can build when someone directs it with a clear idea. I'm still playing with it and watching what comes of it.
Software Keyboard Instrument

C++20, JUCE, CMake · Windows desktop app

A prototype I use myself, started October 2026. Six of seven planned milestones are built.

  • Plays six synth engines written from scratch, a sampled orchestra, and modeled pianos and organs live from a MIDI keyboard, with 115 presets.
  • Records performances as multitrack takes, edited in a piano roll (quantize, humanize, tempo map) and exported as MIDI, WAV, MP3, FLAC, or per-track stems.
  • Turns a folder of audio recordings into a playable instrument through pitch detection, trimming, loop finding, and key mapping.
  • I wrote the spec and milestone plan, made the product and design decisions, and play-tested every milestone. Claude Code wrote the code under my direction. 3,688 automated checks pass across about 27,500 lines of C++.
