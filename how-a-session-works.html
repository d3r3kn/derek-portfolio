How a session works

What happens on the free intro call, what a paid session looks like, and how payment works, before you book anything.

The free intro call

After you send the questionnaire, I email you within two business days to set up a time. The call is free and lasts up to 20 minutes, by phone or video.

We talk about what you want to do and where you're stuck. I suggest a plan and tell you what it would cost. You don't have to book anything.

A paid session, start to finish

Each session is one hour, by phone or video. I don't record our calls.

1. Start with your planWe pick up the plan from the intro call, so we don't start from scratch.
2. I guide, you clickOn video, you share your screen. On the phone, you tell me what you see. You do the clicking, so nothing changes without you seeing it.
3. Try it on your ownBefore we finish, you do the new thing once on your own while I watch, and we note what to try next.

What to have ready

  • The device you want help with, whether a computer, phone or tablet, charged or plugged in.
  • A short list of what you want to get done. A sticky note is fine.
  • A way into your accounts. You type your own passwords, and we pause screen sharing while you do.
  • For video, I email you a link before the session. Nothing to install ahead of time.

If it's not a fit

If what you need isn't something I can help with, I'll tell you on the call. The intro call is free either way.

Payment

We agree on the price on the intro call. You pay before each session, and I'll send a secure payment link when we book. For a set of 3 sessions, you pay once, before the first one.

Sessions are $60, or 3 for $165. For small businesses, $125, or 3 for $345.

What I'll never do

  • Ask for your passwords.
  • Take control of your computer. No remote-control software, ever.
  • Ask you to pay by gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency.
  • Contact you out of the blue. You reach out first.
